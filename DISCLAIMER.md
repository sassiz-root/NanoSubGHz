## 1. Purpose

NanoSubGHz is provided **exclusively for educational, research, and
personal experimentation purposes**. It is designed to help students,
makers, and security researchers understand how sub-GHz ASK/OOK radio
communication works — the same technology used in:

- Remote-controlled garage doors
- Car key fobs
- Wireless doorbells
- Home automation sensors
- Industrial remote controls
- Gate openers and shutters

Understanding how these systems work is essential to **designing more
secure products** and identifying weaknesses in legacy ones.

## 2. Legal Warning

Radio frequency regulations vary **significantly** between countries. In
many jurisdictions, the following activities are **illegal** without
explicit authorization from the device owner and, in some cases, a
regulatory body:

| Activity | Legal risk |
|---|---|
| **Sniffing / intercepting** RF communications | ❌ Illegal in most countries (privacy, wiretap laws) |
| **Replaying** captured codes to operate a device you don't own | ❌ Illegal (unauthorized access) |
| **Jamming** (intentional interference) | ❌ **Criminal offense** in most countries |
| **Brute-forcing** a code to access a device you don't own | ❌ Illegal |
| Capturing your own devices, in a lab | ✅ Usually legal |
| Researching protocols publicly documented | ✅ Usually legal |

By way of example only (not exhaustive):

- **USA** — FCC Rules 47 CFR Part 15; the **Federal Wiretap Act**;
  sale and use of jammers is prohibited under **47 U.S.C. § 333** and
  FCC enforcement actions.
- **EU** — **RED (2014/53/EU)**; national laws forbid unauthorized
  interception and jamming.
- **UK** — **Wireless Telegraphy Act 2006**; jammers are illegal to
  possess, sell, or use.
- **Iran** — Subject to **CRA (Communications Regulatory Authority)**
  regulations; unauthorized RF transmission is prohibited.

> **If you do not know the law in your country, assume the activity is
> illegal until proven otherwise.**

## 3. Safety Warning

**Do not use this device in any of the following situations:**

- 🚫 Near **aviation** or airport infrastructure
- 🚫 Near **medical devices**, hospitals, or ambulances
- 🚫 Near **emergency services** (police, fire, EMS) communications
- 🚫 In vehicles in motion or **traffic safety** systems
- 🚫 Where jamming could endanger **human life**
- 🚫 On devices you do not personally own or have written permission to test

**Never jam in public spaces.** Even if you are testing your own device,
your signal may interfere with nearby systems. Use a **shielded enclosure**
(Faraday box) for any transmission test beyond a few centimeters.
